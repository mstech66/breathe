# Breathe privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Breathe is a desktop app that helps you balance focused work with breaks and guided breathing. It is built to work entirely on your own computer.

## What Breathe collects

**Nothing.** Breathe does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting
- No internet connection is used by the app

## What stays on your computer

Breathe saves a few things locally so it can remember them between launches:

- your settings (for example your rhythm, working hours, sound and notification choices)
- how many focus sessions you've completed today

This information never leaves your computer. Uninstalling Breathe removes it.

## Notifications

Breathe uses your operating system's notification system to remind you about breaks. Notifications are created on your computer and are not sent through any outside service.

## Children

Breathe doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new date.
